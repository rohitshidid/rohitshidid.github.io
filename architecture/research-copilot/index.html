ResearchCopilot

ResearchCopilot answers research questions from arXiv papers and cites its sources. The Next.js frontend calls a FastAPI backend, and pipeline.run_query() (also used by cli.py) plans sub-queries with an LLM, downloads and chunks arXiv PDFs, and indexes them in LanceDB. Dense and BM25 results are merged with Reciprocal Rank Fusion and reranked by a cross-encoder. The LLM (Claude, GPT-4o or Groq) then writes an answer with [n] citations, and Semantic Scholar supplies the citation graph.
