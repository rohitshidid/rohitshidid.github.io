Mutation Simulator

Architecture diagrams generated from the source code

Mutation Simulator teaches red dots to dodge hunters and walls in a circular arena, in two separate static pages that share no code. generation.html evolves a population of tiny neural networks: each genome is scored over several trials, the best are kept and the rest are bred and mutated. reinforcement.html trains a single shared policy with REINFORCE, turning per-step rewards into gradient updates, with a collapse guard that rolls back to the best policy if training falls apart.
