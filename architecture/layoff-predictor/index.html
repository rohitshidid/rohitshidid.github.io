Layoff Predictor

A Flask web app estimates the chance a US public company will announce layoffs soon. When you enter a ticker or company name, predict.py resolves it to a ticker using the NASDAQ/NYSE/AMEX lists, pulls about 400 days of prices from Yahoo Finance via yfinance, and computes six technical signals (3- and 6-month price drop, volatility, RSI, and distance from the 50/200-day averages). A RandomForest saved in model.pkl scores those signals and also builds a 12-month risk timeline. The model was trained offline by train.py on real layoff events (layoffs.csv) against random no-layoff dates.
