Parkinson's Symptom Detector

Mbed OS firmware for the STM32 B-L475E-IOT01A board. It reads the on-board LSM6DSL accelerometer and gyroscope over I2C at 52 Hz, fills 3-second windows, and runs CMSIS-DSP FFTs to find the dominant frequency of each signal. Fixed threshold rules (not a trained model) turn those frequencies into Walking, Freezing of Gait, Tremor (3-5 Hz) or Dyskinesia (5-7 Hz). Each result lights an LED, prints to the serial port, and is sent over BLE to a phone through the custom GATT service 0xA000.

  • System architectureThe IMU -> acquisition -> FFT -> symptom logic chain on the board, and the LED, serial and BLE outputs.
  • Lifecycle: symptom state machineBoot and calibration, the Normal -> Walking -> FOG gait path, the per-window Tremor/Dyskinesia alerts, and the sensor-fail halt.
