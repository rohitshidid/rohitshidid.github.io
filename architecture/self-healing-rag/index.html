Self-Healing RAG

Self-Healing RAG answers questions over a small document corpus, but checks its own work before replying. A question from the Streamlit app goes to SelfHealingRAG, which retrieves chunks from a FAISS index (MiniLM embeddings) and generates an answer with a Groq-hosted LLM. NLI and cross-encoder checks (DeBERTa-v3, ms-marco) catch four failures: low similarity, irrelevant chunks, refusals and ungrounded claims. healing.py then fixes each one (query rewrite, HyDE, wider retrieval, claim re-retrieval), up to 3 times, and every step goes into a healing log shown next to the answer.

  • ArchitectureThe components: Streamlit UI, orchestrator, retriever + FAISS, verifier, healing strategies, Groq LLM, and offline indexing.
  • Workflow: detect-and-heal loopHow one query moves through the three stages, and where each failure check sends it to a healing strategy.
